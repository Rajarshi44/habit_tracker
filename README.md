# GRIND Habit Tracker

GRIND is a premium, no-nonsense productivity and habit tracking platform designed for developers and deep-workers who want to maintain absolute consistency. It forces discipline through a minimalist, editorial-style interface that strips away the fluff of traditional habit trackers.

## 🚀 Core Features

- **The Dashboard**: A brutalist, distraction-free daily view. Mark tasks as done, skipped, or failed. No emojis, no gamification, just pure execution.
- **Dynamic Learning Pipeline**: Set up a sequence of learning stages (e.g., HTML → JS → React). Track your progress percentages. Once a stage hits 100%, the dashboard automatically seamlessly transitions your learning block to the next subject.
- **Subject Rotation**: Automate your college or study schedule. Enter a comma-separated list of subjects, and GRIND will rotate them into your daily study block automatically so you never neglect a topic.
- **AI Daily Briefing & Insights**: Wake up to a personalized, stoic briefing generated by advanced AI based on your actual performance data from the previous week and your current streaks.
- **Streak Recovery & Analytics**: Track your active streaks, view detailed heatmaps of your execution over the last 90 days, and read brutally honest weekly performance reviews based on your consistency.
- **Cloud Synchronization**: Log in from any device. Your entire protocol—tasks, history, pipeline, and settings—is securely synchronized to the cloud in real-time.

## 🛠️ Setup & Execution
1. Clone the repository
2. Install dependencies (`npm install`)
3. Initialize the default protocol from the app settings to get started instantly.
